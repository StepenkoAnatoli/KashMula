## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Playto really free?

Yes. Playto has a 'Forever Free' plan that includes all core features—community, courses, and chats. We only make money when you make money, by taking a low transaction fee (2.5% on cards, 0.2% on bank transfers) on payments. No monthly subscriptions.

### Can I use my own custom domain?

Not yet, but we are working on it! Currently, you get a beautiful \`playto.so/yourname\` link that works instantly on mobile and web.

### How is this different from Lemon Squeezy?

Unlike Lemon Squeezy which charges you monthly fees regardless of your success, Playto is free to start. Plus, Playto is mobile-first, meaning your community lives in a dedicated app, not just a mobile-responsive website.

### Does it work Globally?

Yes! Playto is built for global creators. We support local payments like UPI in India, and card payments globally. You can accept payments from anywhere.
