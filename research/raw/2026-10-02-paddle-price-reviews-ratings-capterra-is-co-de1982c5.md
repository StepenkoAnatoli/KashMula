### "Hidden requirements make approval impossible for new businesses"

20 January 2026




1.0
Show more details

Applied with a legitimate B2B SaaS — registered company, GDPR-compliant policies, DPA, clear terms, instant digital delivery. They requested changes to my refund policy, which I completed within 14 minutes. Then they sent a questionnaire asking for processing statements. When I explained I'm a new business launching for the first time, they rejected me because I have no processing history. This requirement isn't mentioned anywhere on their website or during signup. Wasted over a week in their approval process only to be rejected for something I could never provide. If you're a new SaaS founder, skip Paddle — they don't actually accept new businesses despite marketing to startups.

Pros:


The concept of a merchant of record handling global tax compliance is appealing for SaaS businesses.

Cons:


Hidden approval requirements that aren't disclosed anywhere. They require 3 months of payment processing history to get approved, but don't tell you this until after you've spent a week jumping through hoops. Impossible for new businesses to qualify — classic catch-22.
