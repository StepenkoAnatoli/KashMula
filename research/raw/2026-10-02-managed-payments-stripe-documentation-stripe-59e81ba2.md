# ManagedPayments

## Sell digital products globally with the Stripe merchant of record solution for tax, fraud, disputes, and support.

Ask about this page

Copy for LLM

View as Markdown

Install tools

Use Managed Payments to sell digital products such as SaaS, software, and digital content or downloads. The Stripe merchant of record solution handles sales tax, VAT, and GST compliance in more than 80 countries, along with fraud prevention, dispute management, and transaction-level customer support.

## Unsupported integrations

Managed Payments doesn’t support:

- Stripe [Connect](https://docs.stripe.com/connect) (platform or marketplace integrations)
- Payment flows that use [embeddable web components](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/accept-a-payment?payment-ui=elements&api-integration=checkout) or other [advanced integrations](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/advanced)

- Attaching [invoice items](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/invoices/subscription#adding-upcoming-invoice-items) on a `Customer` object to a Managed Payments subscription
- Generating a [one-off invoice](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/invoices/subscription#generating-invoices) on a `Customer` object or for a subscription outside the billing period

- Creating a subscription outside of Checkout or Payment Links
- Third-party tax integrations
