Are your woolen clothes warm enough for the winter?


We and our children get through the winter with the woolen clothes.Wool regulates heat wonderfully and is breathable.The things are also cut in such a way that you can easily pull something underneath.With a lot of wind and heavy long-lasting wetness, the wool reaches its limits. With a rain jacket / rain pants additionally over it, the problem is already solved.The advantage of strong breathability is when the children are well dressed and then romp and develop their own body heat, this excess heat is directed outside. The children are not so sweaty and then do not freeze wet sweaty in the cold.


How do I care for woolen things correctly?


Wool has unique properties. It is breathable, water-repellent, dirt-repellent, self-cleaning.Wool does not have to be washed often, ventilation is usually enough.But it is very sensitive to heat:Therefore, you must not wash the wool with warm water and also not put on the heater or in the direct warm sun. Otherwise, she contracts and the garment becomes smaller.Instead: Carefully wash COLD by hand (not in the machine), do not rub and walk (then it mattifies even more and becomes smaller).After washing, pull back into shape and hang over a large area to dry.
