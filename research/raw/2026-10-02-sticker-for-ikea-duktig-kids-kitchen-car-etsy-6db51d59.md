### Highlights

- Materials: vinyl, sticker, print, film, wall tattoo, decals




With our customised furniture films, the simple IKEA play kitchen becomes an eye-catcher in the children's room. The high-quality design films are easy to apply and bubble-free.

Give your IKEA DUKTIG children's kitchen a fresh look with this adhesive film!

The film set is perfectly tailored to the surfaces of the children's kitchen and brings a modern design with playful details to your play kitchen. Easy to apply and removable without leaving any residue, it adds a personalised touch that will delight little chefs.

✅ The sticker set contains:

\- Adhesive film for the back wall 32 x 67.5 cm

\- Adhesive film for the right-hand front door 31 x 31 cm

\- 2 adhesive films for the upper small compartments 15.5 x 17 cm

\- Oven window 20.2 x 20.2 cm

\- 2x oven surround (right & left) 5, 1 x 20.2 cm

\- 2x oven surround (bottom & top) 5.1 x 31.2 cm

\- Microwave window 8.8 x 19.9 cm

\- Microwave control panel 5.1 x 8.5 cm

\- Oven control panel 4.5 x 63.8 cm

\- Oven control panel 4.9 x 63.8 cm

Our furniture foils are:

✅CE-certified and without hazardous plasticisers

✅Ecological ink without solvents

✅Bubble-free adhesion

✅Removable

✅Robust and durable

💚With hand and heart from Cologne (Germany)

➕ The following products also match:

\- Cooking plate for children's kitchens

\- Leather handles

\- Wooden handles

💡Please note that the adhesive film is slightly smaller than the surface to be covered. This is intentional, as the sticker is easier to apply and does not stick out.

❕NOTE: You will also need a chipboard measuring 32 x 67.5 cm for the back panel. You can get this for 2-3 € at a DIY store. You can simply attach the board to the back of the play kitchen using small nails.
