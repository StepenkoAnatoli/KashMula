## Startup Insights

- Value proposition: Tracks the signals that move before the price does, such as Reddit chatter, hiring, insider buys and congress trades, and rolls them into one 0 to 100 score for about 3,000 US stocks.
- Problem solved: Investors can't watch dozens of alternative data sources themselves. AltIndex collects and scores them daily and puts the score on their own portfolio, with alerts.
- Pricing model: App: Free tier, Starter $29/mo or $199/yr, Pro $99/mo or $499/yr. API: Hobbyist $19/mo, Developer $79/mo, Research $199/mo, Enterprise custom.
- Target persona: Investors
- Audience type: [Both](https://trustmrr.com/search?targetAudience=Both)
- Team size: 2–5 people
- Funding: Bootstrapped
- Estimated user count: 71,000
- Additional info: iOS, Android and web app. 700+ paying subscribers, 76% on annual plans. REST API plus an AI assistant connector for Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor. Data history back to 2020 or earlier. Includes the AltIndex newsletter.
- Markets: [Fintech](https://trustmrr.com/category/fintech), [AI](https://trustmrr.com/category/ai)
