# Cargo Systems Messaging Service

Welcome to U.S. Customs and Border Protection’s (CBP’s) Cargo Systems Messaging Service (CSMS). CSMS is one of CBP’s methods for communicating to our trade partners news and updates on our automated systems – namely the Automated Commercial Environment (ACE). To receive the CSMS messages via email, please register below.

[Register for CSMS Messages](https://public.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCBP/subscriber/new?topic_id=USDHSCBP_3)
