Many different newsletter publishers who were just getting started have asked me variations of the same question:

> How many subscribers do I need to get sponsors?

My answer is always the same:

> Zero!

They will often be shocked or confused.

Who’s going to pay to sponsor a newsletter with no subscribers?

Nobody will pay to sponsor it, but you can get a sponsor, no problem!

**Ever heard about affiliate marketing?**

If you haven’t, it’s when companies will pay you based on conversions.

Usually, it’s commission based on sales that you send their way, other times it’s a fixed amount for some sort of conversion (like a newsletter signup, app install, etc).

I’ll usually suggest that you find partners that have products you like and that your audience would find useful to promote in your newsletter.

A great way to find some is to Google something like this:

> Your Niche + “Affiliate Program”

Another way is to go to the websites directly and look if they have an affiliate program, or you can contact them if you don’t see it.

There are even affiliate networks where you can find multiple different companies and products to become an affiliate for like:

- JVZoo
- Impact
- Rakuten
- Sharesale
- Clickbank
- WarriorPlus
- PartnerStack
- Commission Junction

On top of that, you can affiliate with some of the biggest online marketplaces like Amazon, eBay, Gumroad, and many others.

There isn’t a niche (topic) that you couldn’t find at least a few products to become an affiliate for and earn some money.

**You can even get paid while you grow your newsletter.**
