# Manage your payouts and invoices

As a Shopify Partner, you can manage your earnings, payouts, and invoices through the Partner Dashboard.

You can receive payouts through various methods including PayPal, bank account, and Wire Transfer. Your available options depend on your country or region and preferred currency. After you've set your payout method and start receiving payouts, you can track your payouts from the Partner Dashboard and review your invoices for any fees. If your Shopify fees are [subject to taxes](https://help.shopify.com/partners/partner-program/sales-taxes), then each applicable tax is listed as a separate line item on the invoice.
