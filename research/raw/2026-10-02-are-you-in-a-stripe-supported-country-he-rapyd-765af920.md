## Before choosing a payment gateway provider, you must first understand if you are located in a Stripe supported country — and what other options you have.

International payments company Stripe is known for its ability to accept payments worldwide. Despite their global reach, there are many countries, and even entire regions, where merchants are unable to use this payment gateway.

Unsure if Stripe is available for businesses located in your country? Then read on to learn where this payment gateway operates, what countries are supported by Stripe, and what other payment solutions exist for international businesses.

### What Countries Does Stripe Support?

### **Is Stripe Supported in your Country?**

Stripe only supports merchants in 46 countries. They primarily support merchants located in Europe and North America, while also offering select coverage in Asia, Oceania and Africa. However, Stripe is not available in the vast majority of Middle Eastern and Asian nations. Additionally, Stripe has limited coverage in Latin America. Merchants and businesses in countries that are excluded or have limited functionality should consider [other options for payment providers](https://www.rapyd.net/network/), such as Rapyd, which supports merchants in 70+ countries including in Latin America.

### Is Stripe Supported in India?

Yes, Stripe is available for merchants located in India. However, certain [features, functionalities and business types](https://support.stripe.com/questions/supported-payment-methods-currencies-and-businesses-for-stripe-accounts-in-india) are unavailable or unsupported.

### What If Your Country Isn’t Supported By Your Preferred Payment Gateway?

Stripe has regional supported country coverage, however, what sets the best providers apart are the range and breadth of countries and international payment methods they can support.

Suppose your company is looking for a payment provider that supports a [significantly wider range of merchant locations](https://www.rapyd.net/products/payments/) while also offering local payment options and currencies. In this case, Rapyd could be the comprehensive solution for all your international payment needs.

Rapyd’s [extensive network of supported countries](https://www.rapyd.net/network/) includes nations from across the Americas, Asia Pacific, Europe, Africa, Oceania, and the Middle East, making it easy for businesses around the globe to pay and get paid.

Merchants in more than 70 countries around the world can accept payments with Rapyd. So whether your international expansion plans require supporting 12 separate bank re-direct methods in Spain, accepting cash in Mexico, or enabling multiple e-wallets in Indonesia, Rapyd makes it all possible.
