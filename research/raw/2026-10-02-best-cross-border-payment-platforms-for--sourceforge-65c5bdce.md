### What are Cross-Border Payment Platforms for Lemon Squeezy?

Cross-border payment platforms facilitate the seamless transfer of money across different countries, enabling individuals and businesses to conduct international transactions efficiently. These platforms provide a secure and cost-effective alternative to traditional banking systems by leveraging digital technology and streamlined processes. They often support multiple currencies, offer competitive exchange rates, and ensure compliance with global financial regulations. Popular use cases include remittances, ecommerce transactions, and international business payments. By reducing transaction times and fees, cross-border payment platforms play a crucial role in connecting the global economy and fostering international trade.
