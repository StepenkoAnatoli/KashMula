## In-house editing vs. outsourcing

If you are reading this article, you are likely already relying on in-house editing or outsourcing to fulfill your background removal needs.

**In-house editing can be quite costly,** especially when considering the expenses of hiring graphic designers and paying for hardware and software. **Turnaround time can also be high,** ranging from **5 minutes to several hours per image,** depending on complexity. Ensuring high-quality and consistent images often requires multiple editing and review stages, further increasing the time required for each image.

**Outsourcing** is another common practice for businesses needing large volumes of images processed quickly and efficiently. However, **turnaround times and costs can also be high.** Some companies may take several days to weeks to complete your order, especially if you requested a large number of images. Pricing can vary widely, typically from **$0.65 to $10 per image.**

Other issues with outsourcing include security, quality, and customer support. For instance, when relying on an outsourcing service, you need to inquire about their **security measures,** image handling and storage practices, and the quality of their customer support. Ensuring the output quality meets your expectations might involve trying different service providers, which can be time-consuming.

## Building your own AI background remover vs. using an external AI-powered tool

If traditional options are too costly and time-consuming, you might consider building your own AI background remover or using an external AI-powered background removal tool like remove.bg.

We will now explore both options in detail, highlighting the potential costs and time required for each solution.

### DIY Approach: challenges and costs

Creating a custom AI tool for background removal demands **substantial investment in time and resources.** Here is an overview of the potential costs involved:

- **Salaries:** Even with developers and data scientists on your team, you must consider the costs of their salaries, which can vary depending on experience and location.

- **Server infrastructure and cloud services:** AI development relies heavily on high-speed hardware, including GPUs and specialized processors, as well as ongoing costs for cloud computing resources.

- **Data acquisition and refinement:** Gathering and refining a large dataset of high-quality images is crucial to the development of an AI model, and can be quite costly and time-consuming.

- **Software and tools:** Licensing fees for specialized software and development tools needed for AI development, as well as costs associated with training the model, such as computational power needed for deep learning algorithms.


One might argue that using open-source solutions can make the cost of building one's own AI tool more affordable. However, it is important to not overlook that maintaining high-quality results and continuously improving and maintaining the model requires significant investment. For instance, a consistent part of the cost will be taken up by ongoing maintenance, which includes training and updating the model in order to retain high-quality results.

Finally, you should keep in mind that **development** **time** can be quite long. Typically, from ideation to deployment, the process takes **6 to 18 months,** including the research, development, testing, and refinement phases.

## Wrapping up: making the right choice for your background removal needs

When deciding between in-house editing, outsourcing, developing an AI tool, or buying one, it's essential to weigh the pros and cons. **In-house editing offers control over quality** but can be **time-consuming and expensive** due to the need for skilled graphic designers and advanced software. **Outsourcing can handle large volumes** quickly but may raise concerns about **cost, security, and quality consistency.**

Developing an AI tool offers **customizability, IP ownership, and compatibility with existing infrastructure** but requires a significant commitment of time and resources and might suffer from a lack of training data and quality output.

On the other hand, **buying an AI tool is cost-effective, requires less time, ensures quality assurance, and includes built-in data security,** although it may lack compatibility and offer limited customization. However, AI tools like remove.bg offer many customization options thanks to their numerous [plugins, integrations,](https://www.remove.bg/tools-api) and [APIs](https://www.remove.bg/api).

Ultimately, the decision to edit in-house, outsource, develop, or buy an AI tool depends on your organization's specific needs, resources, and strategic goals. Therefore, it is imperative to carefully assess all approaches to determine the best fit for your business.
