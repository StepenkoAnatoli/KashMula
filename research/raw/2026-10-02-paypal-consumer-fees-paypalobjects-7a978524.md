# PayPal Consumer Fees

Buying and sending payments can be easy, fast, and secure when using 
PayPal.

## Last Updated: 6, July 2026

You can find details about changes to our rates and fees and when they will apply on our <u>Policy Updates</u> 
<u>Page</u>.  You can also view these changes by clicking ‘Legal’ at the bottom of any web-page and then 
selecting ‘Policy Updates’.

**Domestic:** A transaction occurring when both the sender and receiver are registered with or identified 
by PayPal as residents of the same market.

**International:** A transaction occurring when the sender and receiver are registered with or identified by 
PayPal as residents of different markets. Certain markets are grouped together when calculating 
international transaction rates. For a listing of our groupings, please access our <u>Market/Region</u>
<u>Grouping Table.</u>

**Market Code Table:** We may refer to two-letter market codes throughout our fee pages.  For a 
complete listing of PayPal market codes, please access our <u>Market Code Table.</u>

## Withdrawals Out of PayPal

You can typically withdraw funds out of PayPal by standard withdrawals to your linked bank account or 
eligible cards.  A currency conversion (and <u>currency conversion fees</u>) may also apply.  For withdrawals to 
cards, fees are based on the card currency in accordance with the information that PayPal has at the time 
of the withdrawal (including your selection of the currency, where available). The withdrawal fees are 
deducted from your remaining balance in your PayPal account. The maximum amount you will be able to 
withdraw from your balance takes into consideration the deduction of the Withdrawal Fees from the 
remaining balance. If the fee is not in the same currency as your PayPal account balance from which you 
withdraw the funds, a currency conversion will be made, and you will additionally be charged Currency 
Conversion Fees as set out in the <u>Currency Conversion</u> section.

*Note: For additional listings of withdrawal rates out of business accounts, please visit our* 
*PayPal Merchant Fees Page.*
