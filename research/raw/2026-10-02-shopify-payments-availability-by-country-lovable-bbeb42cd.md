## What this status does not prove

- It does not prove you personally will be approved. Shopify assesses each application and can request documents or decline.
- It does not mean a US LLC creates eligibility. Forming an entity does not by itself grant access to Shopify Payments.
- It does not mean a US bank account creates eligibility. A payout account is a requirement, not a qualification.
- It does not cover product, industry or risk restrictions Shopify applies inside an available country.
- It is a dated capture of official documentation, not a live feed. Check the source link before acting.
