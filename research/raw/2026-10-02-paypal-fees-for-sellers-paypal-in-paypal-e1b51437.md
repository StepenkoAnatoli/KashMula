## Domestic

A transaction occurring when both the sender and receiver are registered with or identified by PayPal as residents of the same market.

## International

A transaction occurring when the sender and receiver are registered with or identified by PayPal as residents of different markets. Certain markets are grouped together when calculating international transaction rates. For a listing of our groupings, please access our [Market/Region Grouping Table](https://www.paypal.com/in/business/paypal-business-fees#popover-modal-groupings).

For India users, we only support international payments.

## Alternative Payment Method Rates

We may allow your customers to choose any of the alternative payment methods (APM) listed in our APM agreement at checkout to pay you for purchases.

For a complete listing of the APMs we support, please see [Annex 1](https://www.paypal.com/in/legalhub/paypal/apm-tnc#annex1) of your market’s APM agreement. You must accept the terms of your market’s [APM agreement](https://www.paypal.com/in/legalhub/paypal/apm-tnc) before receiving APM payments.

Our [Commercial Transaction Rates](https://www.paypal.com/in/business/paypal-business-fees#statement-2) apply when you receive APM funded payments.

## Micropayments

Subject to application and pre-approval by PayPal, you may upgrade an existing account to receive the Micropayment rate. Micropayment rates can be ideal as an alternative to standard commercial transaction rates for businesses that process small payments. If you upgrade to the Micropayment rate, this rate will apply to all commercial transaction payments received into your PayPal account. Please [contact us](https://www.paypal.com/in/cshelp/personal) to apply for the Micropayment rate.
