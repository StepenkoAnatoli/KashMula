# PayPal Consumer Fees

Buying and sending money can be easy, fast, and secure when using PayPal.

In an effort to provide customers access to critical funds, PayPal will be temporarily waiving some of its fees for customers sending funds to Ukrainian PayPal accounts or receiving funds into Ukrainian PayPal accounts until further notice. Exchange rates or fees charged by a customer's card issuer or bank may still apply. Please see the [fees for Ukrainian personal accounts page](https://www.paypalobjects.com/marketing/web/shared/clac/en/paypal-fees/Ukraine-paypal-fees-personal-accounts.pdf) and [this Help Center article](https://www.paypal.com/ua/cshelp/article/expansion-of-services-%7C-paypal-ukraine-help1003) for updates and more information about our services for Ukrainian customers.

## Domestic

A transaction occurring when both the sender and receiver are registered with or identified by PayPal as residents of the same market.

## International

A transaction occurring when the sender and receiver are registered with or identified by PayPal as residents of different markets. Certain markets are grouped together when calculating international transaction rates. For a listing of our groupings, please access our [Market/Region Grouping Table](https://www.paypal.com/in/digital-wallet/paypal-consumer-fees#popover-modal-groupings).

For India users, we only support international payments.

## Selling with PayPal

When you buy or sell goods or services, make any other commercial type of transaction, send or receive a charity donation or receive a payment when you “request money” using PayPal, we call that a “commercial transaction”.

## Withdrawals Out of PayPal

You can typically withdraw money out of PayPal by standard withdrawal/transfers to your linked bank account or eligible debit cards. A currency conversion (and [currency conversion fees](https://www.paypal.com/in/digital-wallet/paypal-consumer-fees#statement-currency)) may also apply.

Note: For additional listings of transfer rates out of business accounts, please visit our [PayPal Merchant Fees Page](https://www.paypal.com/in/business/paypal-business-fees).
