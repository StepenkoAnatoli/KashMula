## Withdrawals Out of PayPal

You can typically withdraw funds out of PayPal by standard withdrawals to your linked bank account or eligible
cards. A currency conversion (and <u>currency conversion fees</u>) may also apply. For withdrawals to cards, fees are
based on the card currency in accordance with the information that PayPal has at the time of the withdrawal
(including your selection of the currency, where available). The withdrawal fees are deducted from your remaining
balance in your PayPal account. The maximum amount you will be able to withdraw from your balance takes into
consideration the deduction of the Withdrawal Fees from the remaining balance. If the fee is not in the same
currency as your PayPal account balance from which you withdraw the funds, a currency conversion will be made,
and you will additionally be charged Currency Conversion Fees as set out in the <u>Currency Conversion</u> section.
Note: For additional listings of withdrawal rates out of business accounts, please visit our <u>PayPal Merchant Fees</u>
<u>Page</u>.
