# Application of Form W-8 Series to a non-US art business

Brooklyn Bridge

## General Overview

With the London:NY art axis, we are increasingly seeing transactions between UK art businesses and US clients. Consequently, we have also seen an increase in the number of queries we 
receive about completion of the relevant forms to allow the US buyers to pay for the works without deduction of withholding taxes. This short guide is intended to summarise the requirements 
and act as a pointer for UK art businesses in choosing the correct forms to complete.

**There are several different** W-8 series forms that Non-US persons are required to provide to 
US payers in order for the non-US person to receive the payment in whole. In the absence of 
a properly completed form the US person would be required to withhold up to 30%. The W-8 
forms include W-8BEN-E, W-8BEN, W-8ECI, W-8EXP and W-8IMY. The type of form to be 
provided depends on the following factors:

#### Type of recipient

• Non-US incorporated Entities complete either **W-8BEN-E** or W-8ECI

• Non-US individuals complete either W-8BEN or W-8ECI

## February 2017 • Non-US non-profit organizations complete W-8EXP

• Non-US non-profit organizations complete W-8EXP

• Non-US partnerships provide W-8IMY (with W-8BEN for each partner) or 
**W-8BEN-E**

#### Type of expected payment

• Passive income such as interest, dividend, rents, royalties etc. W-8BEN or 
W-8BEN-E

• Trading income with respect to the performance of services:

• W-8ECI if services are performed in the US and no treaty benefits are claimed

• W-8BEN or W-8BEN-E if services are performed outside the US or inside the 
US but treaty benefits are claimed

• Trading income with respect to sales of tangible goods to US based customers 
W-8ECI or W-8BEN or W-8BEN-E if treaty benefits are claimed

#### Purpose of form

• Establish foreign status for withholding tax purposes

• Claim tax treaty benefits or certify that it is engaged in US trade or business 
and will file a return

#### Applicable forms to a non-US art and antique business selling artwork to US based customers

A non-US art and antiques business that sells artwork to US based customers realises trading 
income that is US sourced under general US income sourcing principals if the title passes in the 
US. That business has to provide each US buyer with either a completed and duly signed Form 
**W-8BEN-E** or **W8-ECI.**

#### A. Form W-8BEN-E if all of the following apply:

• The art and antiques business is an incorporated business and not a partnership
• The business is incorporated under the laws of a country that has a compre-

hensive tax treaty with the US and is entitled to treaty benefits.

• The business does not have a permanent establishment in the US as defined in the 
relevant tax treaty (e.g. it does not directly employ people or operate through a fixed 
office in the US). For this purpose, under most treaties a permanent establishment 
does not include a US incorporated subsidiary that may have its own office and employees in the US.

• The business is not owned, directly or indirectly by US shareholders that control 
more than 50% of the entity.

#### The purpose of Form W-8BEN-E is to:

a) Establish foreign status

b) Claim treaty benefits under the business profits article effectively stating that the   
trading income is not subject to tax under the tax treaty as it is not attributed to a 
permanent establishment.

#### B. Form W-8ECI if all of the following apply:

• If the art and antiques business is an incorporated business and not a partnership
• The business is either incorporated under the laws of a country that does NOT have 
a comprehensive tax treaty with the US and is NOT entitled to treaty benefits or it 
maintains a permanent establishment in the US as defined in the relevant tax 
treaty (e.g. directly employs people or operates through a fixed office in the US). For 
this purpose, under most treaties a permanent establishment does not include a US 
incorporated subsidiary that may have its own office and employees in the US.

• The business is not owned, directly or indirectly by US shareholders that control 
more than 50% of the entity.

#### The purpose of Form W-8ECI is to:

a) Establish foreign status

b) Certify that it is engaged in US trade or business and will file a US tax return to 
declare its US sourced business income and profits.

## Federal and State tax considerations

Regardless of whether the applicable form is W-8BEN-E or W-8ECI, the sale of artwork or any 
other tangible products to US based customers may trigger an income tax return filing requirement at US Federal and sometimes State level. If the gallery provides its customers with Form 
W-8BEN-E, because it determines that the sales revenue is protected from US taxation under 
the provisions of a tax treaty, then the gallery may be required to file a US Treaty Based Return 
in order to disclose the treaty position.

Alternatively, if the gallery provides its clients with Form W-8ECI  because it is actively engaged 
in US trade or business or the provisions of a tax treaty do not apply, then it may be required 
to file a complete US corporate tax return and declare its US sourced revenues and related 
expenses and pay the related income tax.

The US State tax position should also be carefully analysed as the rules vary from one State to 
another with almost half of the US States not following federal treaties. Thus it is not unlikely 
for a foreign business to become subject to State income tax despite being exempt from federal tax pursuant to a tax treaty.
