Any activity that harms beehiiv’s infrastructure, the Services, or the experience of other users is strictly prohibited. This includes both attacks on our technical systems and misuse of Service features in ways that degrade performance, security, or reliability.

**Unauthorized Data Collection:** The use of any automated means, such as bots or scrapers, to collect information from the Services. This also includes the collection of personal information from other users without their explicit consent.

**Resource Misuse:** Any action that places an unreasonable or disproportionate load on our servers, such as operating anonymous proxy servers, running file-sharing sites, hosting unrelated large files, or launching denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks.

**Circumventing Security:** Any attempt to disable, bypass, or circumvent the security measures we have in place to protect the Services, their users, or data. This includes but is not limited to manipulating user authentication or access controls.

**Malware and Harmful Code:** The distribution, promotion, or hosting of malicious software of any kind is strictly prohibited. **Unauthorized Security Testing:** Conducting penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, or other security research on beehiiv’s infrastructure without prior written authorization is prohibited. ### Address Collection and Contact Lists

To safeguard the integrity of our newsletter Services and respect the privacy of recipients, beehiiv requires all users to obtain **affirmative consent** before sending any emails. Consent must be freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous, and provided through a clear affirmative action, in line with applicable privacy and data protection laws.

The following methods of list collection do not meet beehiiv’s requirements for affirmative consent:

- **Purchased Lists:** where email addresses are obtained from data brokers or vendors, even if marketed as “opt-in” or “consented.” Consent is not transferable, and recipients must provide their own explicit, affirmative consent directly to your publication.

- **Rented or Co-Registration Lists:** where consent is bundled or shared with multiple parties, making it unclear who the recipient is agreeing to hear from.

- **Scraped or Harvested Addresses:** where email addresses are gathered from websites, social platforms, or other public sources without permission or affirmative consent.

- **Third–Party-Provided Lists:** where lists are shared by partners, affiliates, or lead-generation networks, where recipients did not directly opt in to your publication.

- **Blanket or Bundled Consents:** where recipients agree to receive communications from multiple parties without clear identification of each sender.


**Proof of Consent:** beehiiv may request documentation showing affirmative consent (e.g., log files, signup source, timestamp, method). Proof of consent should be maintained and provided to beehiiv if requested.

Recipients must always have a clear and reliable way to withdraw consent to receive emails. ### Sender Identification and Transparency

All newsletters and emails sent through beehiiv must be transparent about who is sending them and must provide accurate information to recipients. To maintain trust and comply with applicable regulations:

- **Physical Address:** Every email must include a valid physical postal address. This may be your current street address, a registered post office box, or a private mailbox obtained through a commercial mail-receiving agency authorized under Postal Service regulations.

- **Subject Lines:** Subject lines must be clear and non-deceptive, accurately reflecting the Content and purpose of the message. Misleading or manipulative subject lines designed solely to increase open rates are prohibited.

- **Header Information:** All header fields (including “From,” “To,” “Reply-To,” and routing information such as the originating domain name and email address) must be accurate and must clearly identify the person or organization that initiated the message. beehiiv prohibits sending messages with materially false or misleading header information.

While promotional Content is allowed in moderation, newsletters that are purely marketing products or services or lead-generation vehicles are not allowed.
