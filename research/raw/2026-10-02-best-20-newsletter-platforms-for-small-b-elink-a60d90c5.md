## **What is a Newsletter?**

A newsletter is a regular email that businesses send to their audience to share updates, stories, offers, or helpful tips. It’s like having a friendly conversation with your customers, right in their inbox.

For small businesses and startups, newsletters are one of the **_easiest and most affordable ways to build relationships_** with the audience. You don’t need a big budget to create newsletters; just good content and consistency are enough. You can use [**newsletters to announce new products**](https://about.elink.io/templates/product-page-templates), share blog posts, give exclusive discounts, or simply remind people why they love your brand.

Instead of sounding salesy, a good newsletter feels like a quick, friendly update, something that your readers actually look forward to opening.

Now that you know what a newsletter is, let’s explore the best 20 platforms to create an amazing newsletter that every small business should know.
