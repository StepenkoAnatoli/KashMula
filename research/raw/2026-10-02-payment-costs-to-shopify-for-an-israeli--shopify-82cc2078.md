#### Topic Summary

Summarized with AI on November 4


An Israeli brand opened a Shopify store and received information suggesting they may not need to pay subscription fees to Shopify.

**Key Points:**

- The store owner shared a screenshot (appears to be payment/billing related)
- They requested confirmation about payment requirements for Israeli-registered stores

**Resolution:**

- Confirmed that Israeli-registered stores do not need to pay Shopify subscription fees
- The responder verified this is correct as long as the store is registered and based in Israel
- Instructions were provided for setting up the store properly to reflect this exemption
